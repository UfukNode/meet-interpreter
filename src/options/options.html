Meet Interpreter

Live speech translation for Google Meet calls. You hear the other person in your language, and they hear you in theirs.

Setup status

  • ElevenLabs API key
  • Anthropic API key (or free translation)
  • Microphone permission
  • Your own voice (optional, recommended)

When everything is ready, join a Meet call, click the extension icon in the toolbar and press "Start translation". Use headphones.
